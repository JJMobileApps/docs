03 / READ A PHOTO

Turn the numbers in a photo
into a growth record.

Read the result display of a digital height meter or scale. This feature does not estimate a person’s height from a full-body photo.

Prepare the recognition models on first use

Select Read height/weight from a photo on the entry form. The first time, the app prompts you to download recognition models. Check your internet connection and available storage, then wait for downloading and verification to finish. Recognition then runs on your device.

A model download is different from an app update.

Models provide the data used for reading photos. App updates install a new app version with changes and fixes. If the app closes during model preparation or repeatedly asks you to download, check the installed app version under Help → Check for updates.

From taking a photo to saving a record

  1. Capture a clear result display

    Photograph a digital height meter or scale while the result is displayed, or choose an existing photo. Keep glare and fingers away from the digits. Include the decimal point and unit.

  2. Align the digits horizontally

    Use rotation, movement and zoom in the photo editor. Make the numbers upright and level rather than sideways or tilted.

  3. Crop closely around the display

    Exclude the device body and unnecessary background while leaving a small margin. Keep every digit, the decimal point and the unit inside the crop.

  4. Compare the recognized values with the original

    Correct any misread digits or decimal points. Check that height and weight have not been mixed up and that the values and units are correct, then apply.

  5. Confirm the date and values on the entry form, then save

    Applying the result is separate from saving the record. Review the final details for the selected child and save the entry.

If recognition is inaccurate

  • Retake the photo without reflections from lights, windows or the phone.
  • Use a clear close-up original instead of enlarging a blurry image.
  • If the display has several rows, make sure the intended result is fully visible.
  • Correct missing decimal points or incorrect digits before applying the result.

You can enter height and weight directly if reading a photo is difficult.

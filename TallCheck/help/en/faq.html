04 / QUESTIONS

When you need a little help

Answers about calibration, measurement, model preparation and managing the app.

Measurement and calibration

The measured height is too high or too low.

Check that the actual paper or marker size matches Settings and that its position matches your reference measurements. Keep the contact point fixed after leveling the phone. Prepare at least two valid reference measurements at different heights, covering the range you will measure. Method 2 also needs distant marker correction.

Can I reuse restored calibration settings on a new phone?

Another device’s level origin and height correction values may cause errors. Set the level origin and repeat reference measurements on your new phone.

The app cannot detect the marker.

Keep the sheet flat and the entire marker visible. Avoid glare and shadows. Check that the selected paper or marker matches the one you are using. See Preparation and calibration for the complete setup.

Reading photos and updating the app

The screen disappears after the model download finishes.

The app may have closed during model verification. Reopen it, check the version in Help and check Google Play for an update. If a fixed version is not yet available to your account, retry after it has been released to you. Back up existing records before clearing any app data.

How do automatic updates work?

The app checks for a new version on a fresh launch. While it remains running, it checks again when you return after 24 hours since the last check. You can also check manually in Help. If an update is available, choose Update to open Google Play’s download and installation screen. The app restarts after installation. You can choose Later or cancel and continue using the app.

I heard there is a new version, but I cannot see it.

Availability depends on the Google Play account you are using. For test releases, check that the Play Store account is the account enrolled in the test. Release availability, rollout progress and installation source may affect the result. If the check fails, use Open Google Play to check directly.

The photo result contains incorrect values.

Make sure the crop includes every digit, decimal point and unit, without glare or tilted text. Compare the result with the original and correct errors before applying it. See Reading a photo for the capture and cropping steps.

Premium access and records

Where can I check my premium status?

Open More → Help. A Premium badge appears beside the version only when premium access is active. For trial status, purchases and purchase restoration, open More → Settings → tallCheck Premium. The 30-day trial starts after the first successful height measurement, photo reading, backup or restore. Direct entry and viewing existing records remain available.

How do I back up my records?

Open More → Settings → Backup/restore. Select the children and whether to include settings, then save a CSV file. Current CSV backups include child information, measurements, built-in avatars and selected settings. Uploaded profile photos are excluded. The file is not encrypted, so keep it in a safe location.

Will restoring a backup erase my existing records?

The restore screen lets you select children and settings, and choose adding profiles or replacing all profiles. For a child with the same name, review the options to merge records, replace records or add a separate child. Replacing all profiles replaces current children and records, so back up first. Older ZIP and .tallcheck backups are supported; enter a password if the backup requires one.

Are purchases and trial dates included in a backup?

Purchase access and trial dates are not part of user-data backups. Restore purchases from premium details using your Google Play purchase account.

Keep the in-app guides handy

The height measurement and photo-reading screens include illustrations, and Settings provides reference measurement guidance. This website describes the current app; names and menu locations can differ in older versions.
